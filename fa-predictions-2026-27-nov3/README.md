# Free agent predictions, 2026-27 (corrected Nov. 3, 2026)

These are my model's predictions for the 2026-27 free agent class, corrected on Nov. 3, 2026. The original Oct. 30 version is kept in fa-predictions-2026-27/ for reference.

**What changed:** My model uses each hitter's position as an input. In the Oct. 30 version, positions for past contracts came from each player's current MLB listing rather than where he played when he signed, so some fielders who later became DHs were counted as DHs. I now use the position each player played most in his platform season, for both past contracts and this class. This mainly affects hitters: 53 of 273 players move more than 5%. Pitchers are essentially unchanged.

**My picks:** My contract-length picks are unchanged except Arozarena's (now 7 years, after he received a qualifying offer); their AAVs are recalculated from the corrected model. For players who received a qualifying offer, my picks use the QO-attached prediction (henry_picks_public.csv has one row per player, so for those eight players, including Arozarena and Lowe, both the model and pick columns are from the QO-attached branch).

**Grading:** Players who received a qualifying offer will be graded against the QO-attached row, since that's the condition that actually holds. Every other player is graded against the no-QO row. This Nov. 3 version is the one I'll grade. It replaces the grading note in fa-predictions-2026-27/README.md, which describes a private file.

**Columns:** The columns are the same as in the Oct. 30 version and are described in fa-predictions-2026-27/README.md. The `v2_*` columns are my model's predictions with the length drawn from its length model rather than fixed.

**Signings:** One player in this class had signed an MLB contract when this version was published: Jacob Webb re-signed with the Cubs (reported Nov. 2, 2026). His prediction is kept here but won't be graded.
